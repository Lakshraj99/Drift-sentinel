# Contributions

DriftSentinel originated as a university group project. Phase 1 established
the original streaming-data, rolling-feature, and reactive-detector foundation.
That foundation was group work and is not claimed as solely authored by Laksh
Raj.

Laksh Raj's continuation completed Phase 2 and Phase 3, including:

- the leakage-safe sequence-modelling pipeline;
- GRU, LSTM, Transformer, and current-batch Logistic Regression models;
- the five-batch predictive target and chronological evaluation;
- event-based warning metrics and ADWIN/DDM/KSWIN comparisons;
- reproducible abrupt and gradual SEA benchmark reconstruction;
- verified INSECTS replay, recovery replay, and alert-triggered adaptation;
- adaptation-cost analysis, experiment reproducibility, and regression tests;
- final project documentation and presentation/viva material.

This standalone repository preserves that academic attribution while packaging
the complete final submission state.
